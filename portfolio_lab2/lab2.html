About Me

I study Computer and AI Engineering at Ain Shams University (Class of 2028) as an Egyptian Pioneers Scholarship recipient. My coursework covers machine learning, algorithms, compilers, operating systems, database systems, and hardware prototyping.
I started in robotics. I graduated from the STEM school system, joined its robotics team as a member, and later became an instructor. That is where I learned to care about the whole stack: a sensor reading is only useful once it becomes a decision, and a model is only useful once someone can use it.
I completed the NTI Machine Learning Summer Training (90 hours of technical training plus a 30-hour freelancing module) and took part in an IEEE CUSB AI workshop and Fab Lab. I also practice competitive programming on Codeforces.
